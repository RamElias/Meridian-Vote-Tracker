Calculation rules

Alignment is 100 × matching Y/N vote weights ÷ all eligible Y/N vote weights. Abstentions (A), absence (X), missing records and non-membership are excluded. A zero denominator is N/A.

Participation is Y/N/A ballots ÷ approved Y/N/A/X member ballots. Coverage is approved member records ÷ selected votes during membership. Missing records are not treated as absence. Percentages are displayed to one decimal place.
